# E750 portal client test — veth client 2026-10-29

## setup
- portal role applied: br-portal 10.0.0.1/24, phy1-ap0, eth0 192.168.8.1 untouched
- nodogsplash gatewayiprange 10.0.0.0/24, gatewayaddress 10.0.0.1, webroot /etc/nodogsplash/htdocs
- package redirect splash restored; portal uhttpd on 2051
- client: veth pair, netns, 10.0.0.100/24, default via 10.0.0.1 (kmod-veth installed)

## results
- splash 2050 from the client: HTTP 200, redirect to the TollGate portal
- portal 2051 /splash.html: HTTP 200
- ndsctl sees the client (ip 10.0.0.100, mac, active)
- pre-auth internet: blocked (ping 1.1.1.1 0/1, curl example.com 000)
- ndsctl auth 10.0.0.100: success
- post-auth internet: still blocked (ping 0/2, curl example.com 000) with BSBF enabled and with BSBF disabled
- firewall: forward_portal accepts to wan, srcnat_wan masquerade present; ndsNET has mark-based drop/accept

## conclusion
- the earlier 2050=500 was a request from the gateway itself; a real client gets 200.
- the captive-portal front door and TollGate SPA work from a client.
- post-auth forwarding does not recover after ndsctl auth; this is the remaining blocker
  (nodogsplash mark/fw4 forwarding interaction), not the AP or the TollGate daemon.
- rolled back for safety; BSBF healthy.
