# Phone disconnect on the 5 GHz portal AP — 2026-10-30

## Symptom
The phone associated, obtained DHCP, then was dropped after ~10 s and fell back
to another network.

## hostapd (5 GHz, phy2)
23:45:15  STA ca:83:c5:81:79:9f authenticated / associated (aid 1)
23:45:15  AP-STA-CONNECTED ca:83:c5:81:79:9f
23:45:19  AP-STA-DISCONNECTED ca:83:c5:81:79:9f / disassociated
23:45:20  deauthenticated due to inactivity (timer DEAUTH/REMOVE)

Earlier attempts show the same pattern (76:1c:1c:cf:28:3c, 5e:28:38:30:3a:5e).
Two leases were issued (10.0.0.159, 10.0.0.160).

## Radio
ieee80211 phy2: Hardware restart was requested
ath10k_pci ... failed to read device EEPROM at 0000
ath10k_pci ... failed to read calibration data from EEPROM: -22
ath10k_pci ... device successfully recovered

5 GHz ran skip_otp (uncalibrated) at txpower 10 dBm.

## Fix applied
Portal moved to the calibrated 2.4 GHz radio (AP+STA on phy1), 5 GHz disabled and
the ath10k driver unloaded:

before: CPU 0% idle, Mem 22 MB free
after:  CPU 90% idle, Mem 41 MB free

iw dev:
  phy1-sta0 hermes-lan ch11
  phy1-ap0  TollGate-E750 ch11 (02:83:c4:4d:fc:3b, 20 dBm)
