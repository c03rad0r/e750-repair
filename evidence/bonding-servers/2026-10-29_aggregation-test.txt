## Parallel 4 x 10 MB
standby:   40 MB in 9 s  = ~35.5 Mbps, split sta=44,790,309 wwan=476
aggregate: 40 MB in 39 s = ~8.2 Mbps,  split sta=27,222,745 wwan=18,311,593

## Interpretation
- Aggregation is mechanically working: both subflows carry payload.
- It is slower than standby: -9% single-stream, ~4x slower under parallel load.
- The 4G RTT is 41-89 ms vs ~29 ms on the STA; the in-kernel `default`
  scheduler (only scheduler available) head-of-line blocks on the slower path.
- The 4G link is also variable and may throttle after volume.
- Decision: keep `wwan0` as a backup subflow (failover). Aggregation stays a
  documented option (`bsbf_backup_interfaces: []`).
