# QCA9887 `skip_otp=1` experiment — 2026-10-29

## Baseline

```text
ath10k_pci 0000:00:00.0: board_file api 1 bmi_id N/A
failed to read device EEPROM at 0000
failed to read calibration data from EEPROM: -22
otp calibration failed: 2
could not init core (-22)
```

## What was tried

The driver exposes `skip_otp` (bool, default `N`). `modprobe`/`modprobe.d`
did **not** apply it on this image; `insmod` did:

```sh
rmmod ath10k_pci; rmmod ath10k_core
insmod /lib/modules/$(uname -r)/ath10k_core.ko skip_otp=1
insmod /lib/modules/$(uname -r)/ath10k_pci.ko
```

`/sys/module/ath10k_core/parameters/skip_otp` became `Y`.

## Result — partial success

The driver initialized and created a 5 GHz phy:

```text
ath10k_pci ...: 10.1 wmi init: vdevs: 16 peers: 127 tid: 256
htt-ver 2.1 wmi-op 2 htt-op 2 cal otp max-sta 128
phy3 -> .../pci0000:00/0000:00:00.0/ieee80211/phy3
```

A 5 GHz AP was configured and **beaconed**:

```text
radio1: band 5g, channel 36, VHT80
phy3-ap0: SSID E750-5G-Test, type AP, 5180 MHz, 80 MHz, txpower 10 dBm
hostapd: phy3-ap0: AP-ENABLED
client scan: SSID E750-5G-Test, Country DE, channels 36-48 @ 23 dBm
```

So the dead QCA9887 can initialize when OTP calibration is skipped. This is the
first time the 5 GHz radio has transmitted since the repair started.

## Instability

After `wifi down`/`wifi up` (and a module reload), the 5 GHz init raced/failed:

```text
ath10k_pci ...: failed to receive initialized event from target: 00000000
failed to read device EEPROM at 0000
failed to read calibration data from EEPROM: -22
radio1: up=false, pending=true
```

The 2.4 GHz STA/portal also dropped during the full wifi restart. The device was
restored by disabling `radio1`, rebooting, re-enabling BSBF and starting the
portal services. Final state: 2.4 GHz STA + AP, BSBF endpoints, portal healthy;
5 GHz disabled.

## What it means

- `skip_otp=1` is a real lead: the radio is not dead at the PCI/firmware level.
- It runs **without per-device calibration**; tx power/regulatory behavior is
  not validated. Do not use it in production yet.
- `modprobe` does not pass the parameter here; `insmod` does. A boot script or
  U-Boot cmdline is needed for persistence.
- The init is sensitive to wifi restart ordering; use `wifi reload` (not
  `wifi down/up`) and avoid module reload while 2.4 is active.

## Next controlled experiments

1. With serial attached, load `skip_otp=1` via `insmod`, then only
   `wifi reload`; do not `wifi down/up`.
2. Bring up the 5 GHz AP on a separate bridge (not management), verify beacon,
   association, DHCP, throughput at low txpower.
3. Try `cal-pci-0000:00:00.0.bin` together with `skip_otp=1` (one variable at a
   time) to see if per-device calibration improves stability.
4. If stable, persist with a boot script (rmmod/insmod) or U-Boot `bootargs`
   `ath10k_core.skip_otp=1`, and use 5 GHz for the portal AP (2.4 GHz STA/BSBF)
   to remove the airtime conflict.
5. Do not leave the 5 GHz AP open on the management LAN.

## Current state

- `wireless.radio1.disabled=1`; no 5 GHz phy at boot.
- 2.4 GHz STA/AP, BSBF, `br-portal`, nodogsplash/TollGate healthy.
