## Observation

The TollGate `upstream_detector` probes the local gateway `192.168.8.254:2121`
and the 4G gateway `10.120.4.133:2121` every 30 s, retries 3x, and logs a
failure each time. Those are not TollGates in this deployment; the probing is
harmless but adds log noise and CPU work on a device with ~10 % idle. Consider
disabling upstream/reseller probing when the router only acts as a gateway.
